# 🚑 Golden Hour Navigator - Driver User Manual

Welcome to the **Golden Hour Navigator**. This tool is designed to save lives by instantly routing you (the ambulance driver or paramedic) to the nearest capable hospital with available beds and doctors.

---

## 1. Finding the Right Hospital
When you open the app, you will see the **Emergency Routing** screen.

### Step 1: Set Your Location
- **GPS (Recommended):** Tap the **"Get my location"** button. The app will securely use your device's GPS to find exactly where you are.
- **Manual (Alternative):** If your GPS is not working, click one of the predefined cities under **"OR SELECT MANUALLY"** (e.g., Sukkur, Khairpur).

### Step 2: Select the Emergency Type
Tap the tile that best matches the patient's condition:
- 🫀 **Cardiac** (Heart attack, chest pain)
- ⚠️ **Trauma** (Accidents, severe bleeding)
- 🔥 **Burns** (Fire or chemical burns)
- 👶 **Maternity** (Labor, complications)
- 🧠 **Neurology** (Stroke, seizures)
- 🩺 **General** (Other emergencies)

### Step 3: Choose Search Mode
- **Nearest:** Finds the absolutely closest hospital that has your selected specialty.
- **Best Match:** Uses our smart AI algorithm to recommend a hospital based on how reliable they are, how many beds they have, and how many doctors are on duty *right now*.

### Step 4: Search & Navigate
Tap **"Find hospitals"**. 
- You will see a list of ranked hospitals and an Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA).
- Tap **"Navigate"** to instantly open Google Maps and get driving directions.
- ⏳ **The Golden Hour Timer:** A 60-minute countdown will begin at the top of your screen. If time drops below 15 minutes, it will pulse red. Hurry!

---

## 2. Using the Grok AI First-Aid Assistant
If you are en route to the hospital and need immediate medical advice to stabilize the patient:
1. Tap the green **Sparkle / AI button** in the bottom right corner of the screen.
2. Type in the patient's symptoms (e.g., *"Patient has severe burns on their arm"*).
3. The AI will instantly reply with short, step-by-step first-aid instructions you can perform in the ambulance.

---

## 3. Reporting a "Turned Away" Incident
Sometimes hospitals claim to have beds, but when you arrive, they turn you away. **You have the power to fix this.**

If a hospital rejects your patient:
1. Go back to the app's search results.
2. Under the hospital's name, tap the red **"Turned Away"** button.
3. This instantly drops that hospital's "Reliability Score" in our system, ensuring future drivers are not routed to a fake available bed.

---

## 4. Finding a Specific Specialist (Doctor Search)
If you need to find a specific doctor rather than just a hospital:
1. At the very top of the screen, tap the **"Doctor Search"** tab.
2. Set your location.
3. Choose the specialty (e.g., Neurology).
4. Turn on the **"Available / On schedule now only"** switch.
5. Tap **"Find specialists"**.
6. The app will show you exactly which doctors are physically on duty *at this exact moment* and which hospital they are currently sitting in.
