# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## The Golden Hour: Emergency Referral Failure Navigator

### 1. Executive Summary
The "Golden Hour Navigator" is a unified web platform designed to interlink different hospitals in a given area. It solves the critical problem of ambulance drivers being forced to make blind decisions that result in patients losing their lives due to a lack of real-time information regarding doctor availability, beds, and medicines. By making the entire healthcare ecosystem transparent and easily accessible in a single application, we can save millions of lives.

### 2. Problem Statement (Based on Initial Analysis)
- **The Core Issue**: There is no unified platform connecting hospitals. Ambulance drivers are confused and lack critical data on where to go. 
- **The Consequence**: Patients lose their lives during transit while searching for the right facility.
- **The Root Cause**: A severe lack of information regarding doctor availability, physical resources (beds), and medical supplies (medicines). 

### 3. The Stakeholders (Who is Affected vs. Who Gains)
- **Negatively Affected (The Victims)**:
  1. **The Patient**: Pays the ultimate price by losing their life due to delays.
  2. **The Patient's Family**: Suffers the loss and emotional trauma.
  3. **The Ambulance Driver & Their Family**: Face immense stress, confusion, and the emotional burden of failing to save a life.
- **The Beneficiaries (The Weak Link)**:
  1. **Dual-Practice Doctors**: Doctors who make themselves "available" at multiple hospitals simultaneously for personal gain. Because they do not manage their time properly, they exploit the lack of transparency, causing the whole system to become weak and inefficient.

### 4. The Solution
- **Unified Web Application**: Implementation of a single unified web platform with interlinked hospital data.
- **Transparent Resource Management**: All resources (beds, medicines, doctor availability) are aggregated into a single, easily accessible system.
- **Actionable Driver Dashboard**: An ambulance driver simply opens the website, searches against the patient's specific condition, and immediately gets data on which hospital is actually equipped to save that life.

### 5. Risk Assessment & Mitigation
- **What Can Go Wrong**: Device compatibility issues and poor internet connectivity in rural areas.
- **Mitigation Strategy**: The application will be built as a highly optimized, responsive Progressive Web App (PWA) that uses low bandwidth and caches essential data, ensuring it remains functional even when the internet connection drops or the device is older.

### 6. Success Metrics
- Reduction in patient transit time (from pickup to admission).
- Complete visibility and tracking of doctor schedules across interlinked hospitals.
- Driver satisfaction and reduced time spent deciding on a destination.
